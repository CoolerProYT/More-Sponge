# The freezer

<ItemSlot id="moresponge:freezer" /> The Freezer is a furnace that runs on cold. It resets hot lava sponges and burnt fire sponges, which a normal furnace cannot.

## Crafting

<RecipeCard id="freezer" />

Any stone that works for a furnace works here too, such as cobblestone, blackstone or cobbled deepslate. Mine the freezer with a pickaxe.

## Using it

The freezer works like a furnace. The top slot is the item to freeze, the bottom slot is fuel, and the result comes out on the right.

- Only items with a freezing recipe fit in the top slot, and only freezer fuel fits in the bottom slot.
- Each item takes **10 seconds**.
- The front lights up while the freezer is running.
- Take out the result to collect the stored experience. Breaking the freezer drops it too.

### Automation

Hoppers and item pipes work the same way as on a furnace:

| Side | Slot |
|---|---|
| Top | Item to freeze |
| Any side | Fuel |
| Bottom | Result (extract only) |

## Fuel

<FuelTable />

Powder snow buckets leave an empty bucket in the fuel slot.

## Freezing recipes

| Input | Output |
|---|---|
| Hot Lava Sponge, and Hot Compressed Lava Sponge 1x–5x | Lava sponge of the same tier |
| Burnt Fire Sponge, and Burnt Compressed Fire Sponge 1x–5x | Fire sponge of the same tier |
