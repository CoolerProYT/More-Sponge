# Snow sponges

<ItemSlot id="moresponge:snow_sponge" /> Snow sponges clear snow layers, snow blocks and powder snow. When soaked they become **frozen**.

## Tiers

<SpongeTable element="snow" />

The base Snow Sponge reaches one block further than the other base sponges and can visit up to 125 blocks.

## Getting them

<RecipeCard id="snow_sponge" />

Snow Sponges are also found in igloos, snowy village houses and ancient city ice boxes, and the Warden drops Compressed Snow Sponge 2x ([Loot](./loot)). The [Snow Sponge Trader](./traders) in snowy biomes sells every tier.

## Frozen sponges

A frozen snow sponge thaws the moment it is placed in **any Nether biome** and turns back into the snow sponge of the same tier.

It also thaws in a regular furnace:

<RecipeCard id="snow_sponge_from_smelting" />
<RecipeCard id="compressed_snow_sponge_5x_from_smelting" />

::: warning
Frozen sponges break like snow blocks: they break quickly but drop nothing unless you mine them with a hoe.
:::
