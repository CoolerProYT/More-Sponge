# Water sponges

<ItemSlot id="moresponge:compressed_sponge_5x" /> The water line builds on the vanilla sponge. The mod adds five compressed tiers above it, each with a wet form.

## Tiers

<SpongeTable element="water" />

The vanilla sponge and wet sponge keep their normal behaviour. Only the compressed tiers are added by the mod.

## Getting them

Craft four sponges into a Compressed Sponge 1x, then keep going. See [Compression](./compression) for every step.

<RecipeCard id="compressed_sponge" />

Sponges also come from shipwrecks and buried treasure, Elder Guardians drop Compressed Sponge 2x ([Loot](./loot)), and the [Water Sponge Trader](./traders) sells every tier.

## Drying

A wet compressed sponge dries the moment it is placed in the **Nether**, just like the vanilla wet sponge. It turns back into the dry sponge of the same tier.

You can also smelt it in a furnace:

<RecipeCard id="compressed_sponge_from_smelting" />
<RecipeCard id="compressed_sponge_5x_from_smelting" />

The furnace works for every tier and takes 10 seconds per sponge.
