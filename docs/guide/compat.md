# JEI

With [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) installed, More Sponge adds two recipe pages.

## Freezing

Shows every freezing recipe with its input, output, experience and freezing time. To open it, look up the uses of the Freezer, or click the arrow in the freezer's screen.

## Freezing fuel

Shows every item that powers the freezer and how many items it can freeze. These match the [fuel table](./freezer#fuel).

Crafting, compression and furnace recipes appear on the normal vanilla pages.
