## Getting them

Lava Sponges are also found in bastion remnants and Nether fortresses, and the Ender Dragon drops Compressed Lava Sponge 2x ([Loot](./loot)). The [Lava Sponge Trader](./traders) in the Nether sells every tier.

## Hot sponges

::: warning
Hot lava sponges burn anything that walks on them, like a magma block. Sneak to cross them safely.
:::

A hot lava sponge cools the moment it is placed in a **snowy biome**, the same biomes where snow foxes spawn (for example Snowy Plains, Snowy Taiga, Grove and Frozen Peaks). It turns back into the lava sponge of the same tier.

Outside of snowy biomes, cool it in a [Freezer](./freezer):
