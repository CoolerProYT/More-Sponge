# FAQ

## My sponge turned soaked but left some water behind

Every sponge has a search limit. In a large lake, lower tiers run out before reaching their full reach. Use a higher tier, or place several sponges. The [range explorer](./guide/sponges#range-explorer) shows how far each tier clears in open water.

## Can I compress wet or hot sponges?

No. Compression and decompression only use dry sponges. Reset soaked sponges first (see [Resetting](./guide/sponges#resetting)).

## How do I reset a hot lava sponge without a snowy biome?

Use a [Freezer](./guide/freezer). A normal furnace does not work on hot lava sponges or burnt fire sponges.

## Why does a hot lava sponge hurt me?

Hot lava sponges burn anything that walks on them, like magma blocks. Sneak across them.

## My frozen snow sponge disappeared when I broke it

Frozen sponges break like snow blocks and drop nothing unless you use a hoe.

## I never see sponge traders

- Check that `/gamerule moresponge:spawn_sponge_traders` is `true`.
- Traders only spawn in [matching biomes](./guide/traders). Plains and forests have no sponge trader.
- There is only one try per in-game day, starting at a 30% chance.
- Spawn eggs work in any biome.

## Does this work with the vanilla sponge?

Yes. The vanilla sponge is the base of the water line, and four of them make a Compressed Sponge 1x. The vanilla sponge and wet sponge are not changed.

## Which versions are supported?

Minecraft 26.1+ on Fabric and NeoForge.
